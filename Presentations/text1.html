Laminated Root Rot (LRR)

  • Coniferiporia sulphurascens (Basidiomycota:Agaricomycetes)
  • Several coniferous hosts
  • Both saprophytic and parasitic
  • Spread through root to root contact between trees and/or stumps
  • Disease of the site, for 50 years (Hansen, 1976)
  • Root rot pockets

Sturrock & Garbutt, 1994
