Woods Creek Study Area

  • Mature mixed Douglas fir forest, ~65y old at establishment in 1985. High LRR incidence
  • Network of 15 stands and 75 plots over 162 hectares
  • Thinning treatments applied to stands ranging from ~6-14 hectares.
  • Circular 1/25 hectare sample plot , 11.3 m radius.
  • Measured aprox. every 5-10 years
  • Data recorded was species, diameter at breast height (DBH), and tree health condition.
