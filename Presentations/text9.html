Apiary Study Area

  • Newly planted Douglas fir following clearcut of mixed Douglas fir forest with high LRR incidence
  • Network of 25 stands and 125 plots over 14 hectares in Columbia County in far NW Oregon
  • Roation treatments applied to stands
  • Stands blocked by initial LRR incidence
  • Circular 1/50 hectare sample plot , 8 m radius.
  • Measured sporatically, every 8-24 years
  • Data recorded was diameter at breast height (DBH), and tree health condition.
