Research Questions

  • Do thinning treatments in mature stands with high incidence of laminated root rot increase tree growth and/or reduce mortality?
  • Do rotations of non-host hardwood species in areas with high LRR incidence increase growth and reduce mortality in subsequent Douglas fir plantings?
  • How well do the Forest Vegetation Similator and its Western Root Disease Model variant predict long term growth in these stands?
